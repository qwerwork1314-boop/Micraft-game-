# VitalFlow 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

_最后更新 / Last updated: 2026-11-27_

## 中文

VitalFlow 是一款“本地优先”的健康与健身应用。

1. **我们访问的数据**：经你授权后，VitalFlow 从 Apple“健康”读取心率、静息心率、心率变异性（HRV）、血氧、呼吸频率、手腕温度、睡眠（含睡眠阶段）、步数、步行跑步距离、活动与静息能量、锻炼分钟数、体能训练、最大摄氧量、日照时间、体重和饮水量，并写入你主动记录的饮水量以及在 Apple Watch 上开始的体能训练。
2. **处理位置**：所有分析都在你的 iPhone 和 Apple Watch 上完成。没有 VitalFlow 服务器。
3. **存储**：设置、缓存的每日评分、助手对话保存在本机，使用 iOS 文件保护（NSFileProtectionComplete）。小组件和表盘复杂功能只获得汇总评分。可选的外部 AI API 密钥保存在本机钥匙串（仅限本设备）。
4. **分享**：默认不分享任何数据。无账户、无广告、无分析统计、无追踪。健康数据绝不出售、绝不用于广告、绝不提供给数据经纪商。
5. **可选外部 AI 助手**：默认关闭。仅在你阅读并明确同意后启用。只发送四舍五入的汇总数字（如恢复分数、睡眠小时数、静息心率与 HRV 平均值）以及你的问题，不发送原始样本、姓名、出生日期、设备标识、位置或精确时间戳。关于诊断或用药的问题不会被发送。数据经 HTTPS 发送给 Anthropic，并受其隐私条款约束。你可随时关闭，密钥会被立即删除。
6. **你的控制权**：可随时在“健康”App 中修改权限；在 VitalFlow“设置 → 删除所有 VitalFlow 数据”中清除本应用在设备上存储的全部内容。
7. **非医疗用途**：VitalFlow 的评分为实验性健康估算，不能用于诊断、治疗或预防任何疾病。
8. **儿童**：本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童。

## English

1. **Data accessed** — with your permission VitalFlow reads heart rate, resting heart rate, HRV, blood oxygen, respiratory rate, wrist temperature, sleep (including stages), steps, walking/running distance, active and resting energy, exercise minutes, workouts, VO₂ max, time in daylight, body weight and water from Apple Health, and writes water you log and workouts you start on Apple Watch.
2. **Processing** — all analysis happens on your iPhone and Apple Watch. There is no VitalFlow server.
3. **Storage** — settings, cached daily scores and assistant conversations stay on device with iOS file protection. Widgets/complications receive summary scores only. An optional external-AI API key is kept in the device-only Keychain.
4. **Sharing** — none by default. No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Health data is never sold, used for advertising or given to data brokers.
5. **Optional external AI assistant** — off by default; enabled only after explicit informed consent. Only rounded aggregate numbers and your question are sent (no raw samples, names, birth dates, identifiers, locations or precise timestamps); medical/medication questions are never sent. Data goes over HTTPS to Anthropic and is subject to its terms. Turning it off deletes the key immediately.
6. **Your controls** — manage permissions in the Health app; erase everything VitalFlow stored via Settings → Delete all VitalFlow data.
7. **Not medical advice** — scores are experimental wellness estimates.
8. **Children** — not directed to children under 13.
